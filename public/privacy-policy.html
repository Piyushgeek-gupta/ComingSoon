NOOKAA Beverages & Beyond

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

At Nookaa, we care about the drinks we serve and the people we serve them to. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Nookaa app, website and outlets (together, the “Platform”), why we collect it, and the choices you have. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), Nookaa is the Data Fiduciary and you are the Data Principal.

This policy is issued in line with the DPDP Act and the DPDP Rules, 2025, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your mobile number (required, verified by OTP) and email address (optional).
  • Order details: outlet and table, drinks ordered, customisations such as size, milk, sugar and ice, order notes, and your order history.
  • Membership and rewards details: your Membership Pack and its usage, Nooks earned and redeemed, wallet balance, cashback and coupons used.
  • Payment details: transaction reference, amount, payment method type and status. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by our licensed payment partners; Nookaa never sees or stores them.
  • App activity (only with your consent): drinks you view, items added to cart but not ordered, searches, and how you use app features.
  • Device details: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, notification token and crash reports.
  • Feedback: ratings, reviews and messages you send us.

We do not track your GPS location. We do not collect sensitive information such as health, biometric, religious or bank account details. If you mention an allergy or dietary need in an order note, we use it only to prepare that order.

2. How we use your information

  1. To create and secure your account.
  2. To take, prepare and serve your order, just the way you like it.
  3. To process payments, refunds and Membership Pack renewals.
  4. To run Nooks, cashback, coupons and your wallet.
  5. To send OTPs, order updates, receipts and renewal reminders.
  6. To help you when you contact support.
  7. To prevent fraud, coupon misuse and other abuse.
  8. To understand which drinks and features people enjoy, and to improve our menu and app (with your consent).
  9. To suggest drinks you might like in “Suggested for you” (with your consent).
  10. To tell you about new drinks, offers and items left in your cart by WhatsApp or app notification (with your consent).
  11. To meet tax, accounting and other legal requirements.

We use your data either with your consent or for a legitimate use allowed under Section 7 of the DPDP Act, for example when you give us your number to place an order. We will not use your data for a new purpose without telling you first.

3. Drink suggestions

“Suggested for you” shows drinks based on your past orders, your preferences and what’s popular. Over time we may make these suggestions smarter using your order history and app activity, and we will update this policy before doing so. Suggestions only change the order in which drinks appear. They never change prices. You can switch them off at any time in the app’s privacy settings.

4. Your choices

We ask for your consent separately for app analytics, personalised suggestions, WhatsApp offers and promotional notifications. None of these is switched on by default. You can change them at any time in the app’s privacy settings, reply STOP to any WhatsApp message, or turn off notifications on your phone. You can still order normally if you say no. Service messages such as OTPs, order updates, receipts and renewal reminders will always be sent.

5. Who we share information with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only when needed, with:

  • payment partners licensed under Reserve Bank of India rules, to process payments, refunds and Membership Pack renewals;
  • technology service providers that host our app and data, send OTPs and messages, deliver notifications and help us understand app performance;
  • government authorities, when required by law or a court order;
  • a successor business, if Nookaa is merged, acquired or restructured.

All service providers may use your data only to provide their service to Nookaa, under contracts that require them to keep it confidential and secure.

6. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored on secure cloud servers. If any data is processed outside India, we do so only as permitted under the DPDP Act and with appropriate safeguards.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active and erase it after you delete your account. Order, payment, membership and wallet records are kept for up to 8 years as required by tax and accounting laws. App activity data is deleted or anonymised once it is no longer needed. Security logs are kept for at least one year as required by law. We will give you at least 48 hours’ notice before erasing an inactive account.

8. Your rights

You can:

  • see a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update your information;
  • delete your account and ask us to erase your data, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw any consent you have given;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity;
  • raise a complaint with us and, if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

You can do this through the app’s settings or Help & Support. To delete your account, see how to delete your account. We will respond within the time required by law.

9. Keeping your data safe

We use reasonable security safeguards, including encryption, OTP login, restricted staff access and regular backups. Never share your OTP with anyone; Nookaa staff will never ask for it.

10. Data breaches

If a data breach affects your personal data, we will inform you promptly and report it to the Data Protection Board of India and CERT-In as required by law.

11. Children

Nookaa accounts are for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without verifiable parental consent, and we never track or send targeted offers to children. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we will delete it.

12. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to work properly and, only if you agree, analytics cookies. You can manage cookies through the cookie banner or your browser settings.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on the Platform, and we will notify you in the app of any significant changes.

14. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact us through Help & Support in the Nookaa app. The name and contact details of our Grievance Officer are displayed in the app and on our website. We will acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within 30 days.
