NOOKAA Beverages & Beyond

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nookaa (“Nookaa”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Nookaa app, website and outlets (together, the “Platform”). Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), we are the Data Fiduciary and you are the Data Principal.

This policy is issued in line with the DPDP Act and the DPDP Rules, 2025; the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it; and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

1. Information we collect

  • Phone number (required): used to create your account and verify you by OTP.
  • Email address (optional): used for receipts and account recovery if you choose to add it.
  • Order information: outlet and table, items ordered, customisations, order notes, time and order history.
  • Pass, wallet and rewards information: monthly pass details and usage, wallet balance and transactions, loyalty points and cashback.
  • Payment information: payment ID, amount, payment method type and status. Card, UPI and bank details are entered directly with our payment partner, Razorpay, and are never stored by Nookaa.
  • In-app activity (only with your consent): screens and items viewed, items added to cart but not purchased, searches, taps and notification opens.
  • Device information: device model, operating system, app version, IP address, app identifiers, push notification token and crash logs.
  • Support and feedback: messages, ratings and reviews you send us.

We do not collect your precise GPS location, and we do not ask for sensitive information such as health, biometric, religious or bank account details.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  1. create, verify and secure your account;
  2. take, prepare and serve your dine-in orders;
  3. process payments, refunds and monthly pass renewals;
  4. run the wallet, loyalty points and cashback;
  5. send OTPs, order updates, receipts and pass renewal notices;
  6. provide customer support and handle complaints;
  7. prevent fraud, misuse and security incidents;
  8. understand how the app is used and improve our menu and services (with your consent);
  9. show personalised “Suggested for you” recommendations (with your consent);
  10. send offers, new-drink announcements and cart reminders by WhatsApp and push notification (with your consent);
  11. comply with tax, accounting and other legal requirements.

We process your data either with your consent or for a legitimate use permitted under Section 7 of the DPDP Act, such as when you provide data to place an order or where the law requires it. We will not use your data for a new purpose without informing you and, where required, asking for your consent.

3. Recommendations

“Suggested for you” currently uses simple rules based on your past orders and popular items. In future we may use a machine-learning recommendation system based on order history and in-app activity, and we will update this policy before doing so. Recommendations only affect which items are shown to you first. They never change prices. You can turn them off at any time in the app’s privacy settings.

4. Your consent and choices

We ask for your consent separately for analytics, personalised recommendations, WhatsApp marketing and promotional push notifications. None of these options is pre-selected. You can change your choices at any time in the app’s privacy settings, reply STOP to any WhatsApp message, or turn off notifications on your device. Withdrawing consent does not affect your ability to order. OTPs, order updates, receipts and renewal notices are essential service messages and will continue to be sent.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Razorpay, to process payments, refunds and recurring pass payments;
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS), which hosts our servers and databases;
  • Google Firebase, for login, analytics, crash reporting and push notifications;
  • MSG91, to send OTP messages;
  • WhatsApp messaging providers, to send order updates and, with consent, promotional messages;
  • government authorities, where required by law or a court order;
  • a successor business, if Nookaa is merged, acquired or restructured.

Our service providers may use your data only to provide services to us and must keep it secure.

6. Storage and transfers

Your data is stored on AWS and Google Firebase infrastructure. Where any data is processed outside India, we do so only as permitted under Section 16 of the DPDP Act and with appropriate safeguards.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active and erase it after you delete your account, except where we must keep it longer. Order, payment, pass and wallet records are kept for up to 8 years as required by tax and accounting laws. In-app activity data is deleted or anonymised once it is no longer needed. Security logs are kept for at least one year as required by the DPDP Rules, 2025. Before erasing an inactive account, we will notify you at least 48 hours in advance.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access a summary of your personal data and how it is used;
  • correct, complete or update your data;
  • request erasure of your data or delete your account;
  • withdraw consent for optional uses;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity;
  • raise a grievance with us and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

You can exercise these rights through the app’s settings or by contacting us through Help & Support. We will respond within the time required by law.

9. Security

We protect your data with reasonable security safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest, OTP-based login, restricted access and regular backups. Please do not share your OTP with anyone. Nookaa staff will never ask for it.

10. Data breaches

If a data breach affects your personal data, we will inform you promptly and report it to the Data Protection Board of India and CERT-In as required by law.

11. Children

Nookaa accounts are intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without verifiable parental consent, and we do not track or target advertising at children. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we will delete it.

12. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to function and, only with your consent, analytics cookies. You can manage cookies through our cookie banner or your browser settings.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on the Platform and notify you of significant changes in the app.

14. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact us through Help & Support in the Nookaa app. The name and contact details of our Grievance Officer are displayed in the app and on our website. We will acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within 30 days.
