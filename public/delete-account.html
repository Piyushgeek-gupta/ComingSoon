Delete Your Nookaa Account & Data

Last updated: 7 October 2026

If you wish to permanently delete your Nookaa account and the personal data linked to it, you can do it in the app or by sending us a request. Please read what happens to your wallet, pass and rewards below before you go ahead.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Nookaa app and sign in with your mobile number.
  2. Go to Settings and choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm with the OTP sent to your registered mobile number.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Use this if you can no longer open the app or have lost access to your account.

  1. Send an email to support@nookaa.in with the subject line Delete My Account.
  2. Include the mobile number registered to your account (and your email address, if you added one).
  3. We will acknowledge your request within 48 hours, verify that it is you, and delete your account within 7 business days.

What data will be deleted

  • Your profile information (mobile number, email address, name)
  • Your saved preferences, privacy choices and “Suggested for you” data
  • In-app activity data, such as items viewed, searches and notification opens
  • Device information linked to your account, including your push notification token
  • Your support messages, ratings and reviews

What happens to your wallet, pass and rewards

  • Wallet balance: money you added to your wallet is refunded to your original payment method.
  • Monthly pass: auto-renewal is cancelled and no further charges will be made.
  • Points and promotional credit: unused loyalty points, cashback and promotional credit lapse and have no cash value.

What we are required to keep

Some records must be kept by law even after your account is deleted:

  • Order, payment, pass and wallet records are kept for up to 8 years for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Security logs are kept for at least one year as required by the DPDP Rules, 2025.

We keep these only for the legal purpose and for no other use. For details, see our Privacy Policy.

Additional notes

Once deleted, your account, order history, wallet and rewards cannot be restored. If you come back to Nookaa later, you will need to create a new account. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account, so please use one of the options above.
